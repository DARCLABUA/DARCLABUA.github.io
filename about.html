What the D.A.R.C. Lab does

An undergraduate research lab studying the darknet and cybercrime, run as two teams with one mission.

Mission

Research and explore cyberspace while building the next generation of cyber talent

We study how the darknet is used and how cybercrime works. Along the way, every member gets three things.

Academic research

Take part in real projects on the dark web, cybercrime and social experiences online.

Real-world IT and cyber

Hands-on work with network technology, IT tools, lab assessments, inventory and problem solving.

Hands-on project support

Build and run what active research projects need to get done.

Teams

Two teams, one lab

Join the side that fits you. Researchers produce the work; Implementations makes sure they have what they need.

Where we're headed

A cybercrime research collective for all of UA

Our long-term vision is The University of Alabama Cyber Crime Research Collective (UACCRC): a service that gives faculty the resources to run their own research.

For faculty

The people, computers and hardware professors need to power their own research.

For undergraduates

Work on many angles of cybercrime research, plus real-world IT and cyber experience.
