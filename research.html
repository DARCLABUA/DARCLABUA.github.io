Project Researchers

The research side of the lab. We study the darknet and cybercrime and turn what we find into professional academic work.

What we study

Research areas

The dark web

How hidden services and anonymity networks are used, and by whom.

Cybercrime

How online crime works, how it spreads and how it can be understood and countered.

Social experiences online

How people behave, connect and are affected in digital spaces.

What we do

From question to publication

Members take part in the full research process, alongside our faculty sponsor.

  1. Explore topicsFind open questions worth answering and review what's already known.
  2. Gather dataCollect and organize the data a project needs.
  3. Write it upAuthor professional academic papers that hold up to review.
  4. Present and publishShare findings and work toward publication.
